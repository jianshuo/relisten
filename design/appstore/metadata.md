## 简体中文（主语言）

**名称**（≤30）: Relisten - 英语精听播放器

**副标题**（≤30）: 一句一句听，听不清就再来一遍

**宣传文本**（≤170）:
看美剧、听播客练英语，最怕一句没听清就过去了。Relisten 把每句字幕变成可以跳转、重听、循环的单元，盲听时按住一个键才看字幕或中文翻译。

**描述**:
Relisten 是专为英语听力练习设计的 Mac 播放器。打开一个带字幕的视频或音频，每一句字幕就变成一个可以单独跳转、重听和循环的单元。

一句一句地听
• ← / → 跳到上一句、下一句，↓ 重听当前这句
• 单句循环：同一句反复播放，可设循环次数
• 逐句暂停：每句播完自动停下，方便跟读和复述

先用耳朵，再看字幕
• 盲听模式默认模糊字幕，按住 ↑ 才显示英文
• 按住 C 临时看中文翻译，⇧C 让翻译常显
• 自动识别内封的简体中文字幕作为翻译轨

把最难的半句抠出来
• R：把刚才 3 秒设为片段，不停重复
• A：标记起点和终点，精确循环一小段
• 0.5x – 2x 变速，音调不变

为练习而设计
• 支持 mp4、mov、mkv、mp3、m4a 等格式，读取内封字幕或同名外挂 .srt / .vtt / .ass
• 收藏听不懂的句子，一键跳回原片复习
• 自动记住每个文件的播放进度
• 所有快捷键都可以自定义

隐私
Relisten 不联网、不收集任何数据。你的视频、字幕和收藏都只留在你的 Mac 上。

Relisten 内置 FFmpeg（LGPL v2.1+）用于读取 mkv 等格式。

## English (U.S.)

**Name**: Relisten – Listening Player

**Subtitle**: Replay every line until it clicks

**Promotional text**:
Learning English from shows and podcasts? Relisten turns every subtitle line into something you can jump to, replay and loop — and keeps the subtitles hidden until you hold a key.

**Description**:
Relisten is a Mac player built for English listening practice. Open any video or audio file with subtitles and every line becomes a unit you can jump to, replay and loop.

Line by line
• ← / → previous / next line, ↓ replay the current line
• Loop a single line, as many times as you like
• Pause after each line for shadowing and dictation

Ears first, subtitles second
• Blind mode blurs subtitles; hold ↑ to peek
• Hold C to see the translation, ⇧C to keep it on
• Embedded Simplified Chinese tracks are picked up automatically as translations

Drill the hardest half-sentence
• R loops the last 3 seconds
• A marks a precise A–B fragment
• 0.5x – 2x speed with natural pitch

Built for practice
• mp4, mov, mkv, mp3, m4a and more; embedded subtitles or matching .srt / .vtt / .ass files
• Save hard lines to favorites and jump back to them
• Remembers where you left off in every file
• Every shortcut is customizable

Privacy
Relisten never goes online and collects no data. Your media, subtitles and favorites stay on your Mac.

Relisten bundles FFmpeg (LGPL v2.1+) to read formats such as MKV.
